elio, the donkey. small, stubborn about your limits.

,,//,,,,//,,,,//,,

what

elio manages a small portfolio of tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. it watches prices and your holdings, notices when your mix drifts from the allocation you chose, and suggests trades to bring it back. you approve them, or let elio trade on its own inside strict limits.

how it works

watch prices
     |
check drift ----- within targets --> hold
     |
suggest trade
     |
check limits ----- fails a cap ---> blocked
     |
execute
     |
report ----------- kill switch always armed

example

you choose a target mix. elio holds it there.

apple   ++++++++++··········  50%
nvidia  ++++++··············  30%
cash    ++++················  20%

nvidia runs to 40% --> trim nvidia, buy apple

until the mix is back where you set it. every step shown, nothing hidden.

limits

every trade passes through your caps before it goes anywhere. a per-trade cap, a daily cap, and a loss limit. if a trade fails any of them, it does not happen. nothing moves without your limits. and there is a kill switch: one action stops elio completely.

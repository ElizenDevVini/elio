portfolio agent

ELIO

designed for robinhood chain

1 agent (watches, rebalances,
trades within your limits)

what

  
elio manages a small portfolio of tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. it watches prices and your holdings, notices when your mix drifts from the allocation you chose, and suggests trades to bring it back. you approve them, or let elio trade on its own inside strict limits.


  
how it works


watch prices
     ,,
check drift ----- within targets --> hold
     ,,
suggest trade
     ,,
check limits ----- fails a cap ---> blocked
     ,,
execute
     ,,
report ----------- kill switch always armed


  
example

  
a page from the ledger. every trade elio makes, and every one it refuses, is written down like this.


+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+
·  one afternoon in the meadow           ·
·                                        ·
·  14:02  nvda drifted to 40%            ·
·  14:02  trim nvda -5%          ok      ·
·  14:03  feed aapl +5%          ok      ·
·  14:19  nvda drifted to 45%            ·
·  14:19  trim nvda -15%         blocked ·
·         exceeds the per-trade cap      ·
·  14:19  trim nvda -5%          ok      ·
·  15:04  balanced. elio grazes.         ·
+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+·+

  
the big trade got refused, so elio took the small one instead. limits are not advice. they are walls.


  
limits

  
elio lives inside a paddock you build.


|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|

  
every trade passes through your caps before it goes anywhere. a per-trade cap, a daily cap, and a loss limit. if a trade fails any of them, it does not happen. nothing moves without your limits.


|----|----|----|--  gate: the kill switch  --|----|----|----|

  
one action opens the gate, and elio stops completely.
